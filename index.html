Focus technique

Not sure? New to timers or struggling to start → Pomodoro. Studying or writing → 50 / 10. Deep project and already in flow → 90 / 20.

Immersion

How much the scene moves while you focus. Breaks always bring it fully back to life, because a calming view helps your attention recover.

Behaviour

Why work in intervals?
  • Attention fades with time on task. Performance on sustained tasks drifts downward (the “vigilance decrement”); planned breaks let you come back fresher instead of grinding on tired.
  • A visible finish line helps you start. Short, timeboxed blocks lower the barrier to beginning, which is why Pomodoro works so well against procrastination.
  • Rest your eyes. The 20‑20‑20 rule: every 20 minutes, look about 6 m (20 ft) away for 20 seconds.
  • Make breaks real breaks. Stand, move, hydrate, look out a window. Scrolling your phone keeps your brain busy and makes it harder to restart.
  • Steady ambient sound can mask distracting noises and conversations. Keep it at a low, comfortable level.
