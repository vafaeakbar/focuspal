Privacy policy

Applies to the FocusPal website (focuspal.ajeer.bh) and the FocusPal apps for iPhone and Android. Last updated 30 October 2026.

In short: FocusPal has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send us anything about you.

What FocusPal keeps, and where

Your settings, tasks, focus statistics, chosen place, animal and language are saved only on your own device (in your browser's storage on the website, or in the app's storage on your phone). We never receive them. Clearing your browser data or deleting the app removes them.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or login, so we hold no names, emails or passwords.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking code.
  • No microphone, camera, contacts or location access.

Notifications and speech

If you allow it, the apps show a break alert when a session ends. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us. The captain's announcements in the Airplane Cabin are fixed sentences read aloud by your device's own voice; nothing is recorded.

Services that FocusPal relies on

Like any website, the hosting provider that serves focuspal.ajeer.bh receives standard technical information when you load it, such as your IP address and browser type. FocusPal's typefaces load from Google Fonts, so Google receives the same kind of request data. On the website, the optional "Buy us a karak" button opens Ko-fi, whose own privacy policy applies there.

Children

FocusPal is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
